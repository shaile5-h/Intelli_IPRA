## [1.0.0] - 2026-05-18

### Added
- **Core Engine**: Initial release of the Intelligent Invoice Processing & Reconciliation Agent.
- **LLM Integration**: Seamless extraction of invoice data using Google Gemini 1.5 Flash.
- **PO Matching**: Automated reconciliation against Purchase Order datasets.
- **Anomaly Detection**: Real-time flagging of amount variances (>5%), missing PO references, and duplicate invoices.
- **REST API**: FastAPI-based endpoints for invoice upload, reconciliation, and reporting.
- **Validation**: Business rule validation for ISO dates, currency codes, and numeric values.
- **Developer Tools**: 
    - `server.py` for easy local development with hot-reload.
    - Comprehensive `.gitignore` and `requirements.txt`.
    - Detailed `README.md` with architectural overview and setup guides.
